

## Part 2:

**Cue Card Topic:** {{Describe a good friend who is important to you.}}


- Who he/she is
- How/Where you got to know him/her
- How long you have known each other
- And explain why he/she is important to you


**My Answer (Bullet Point Outline):**

One of my closest friends is a guy named Daniel. We first met during our first year at university, when we were assigned to the same dormitory. At the beginning, we were just casual acquaintances, but after spending late nights chatting and studying together, we gradually became best friends.

I have known him for almost eight years now, and throughout this period he has always been reliable and supportive. For example, when I was struggling with a tough economics course, he patiently explained concepts to me and encouraged me not to give up. On the other hand, whenever he faced personal problems, I tried to be there for him as well. This mutual support has really strengthened our bond.

Daniel is important to me because he is one of the few people who truly understands me. He never judges me when I make mistakes and always gives honest advice, even if it’s not what I want to hear. I admire his sincerity and sense of humor, which makes spending time with him enjoyable. Most importantly, I know that no matter what happens in life, he will stand by me.

To sum up, Daniel is not just a friend but almost like family to me. Having such a trustworthy and caring person in my life is something I value greatly.
